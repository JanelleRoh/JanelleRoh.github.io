Business Administration📚

I am a first-year pre-business student at the University of California, Riverside with a strong interest in marketing, digital communication, and organizational leadership. I am particularly interested in how businesses use social media and online platforms to engage audiences, build brand identity, and communicate effectively. Through both my academic coursework and hands-on experience, I am developing skills that support my goal of pursuing a career in business, with a focus on marketing or management. One of my most valuable experiences comes from managing social media accounts for my high school’s Junior Air Force ROTC program. In this role, I was responsible for overseeing and maintaining our unit’s Instagram and YouTube platforms. I created, edited, and scheduled posts that highlighted events such as drills, community service activities, and ceremonies. This experience allowed me to develop a strong understanding of content creation, audience engagement, and brand representation.

Managing these accounts required consistency, creativity, and attention to detail. I analyzed engagement metrics such as likes, views, and interactions to understand what types of content resonated most with our audience. Based on this, I adjusted our posting strategies to improve visibility and participation. Through these efforts, I contributed to strengthening our program’s online presence and increasing engagement within our school community. In addition to content creation, I collaborated closely with other cadets and instructors to gather information and media from events. This helped me build strong communication and teamwork skills, as well as the ability to manage multiple responsibilities and meet deadlines. I also gained experience using digital tools for photo and video editing, further enhancing my technical and creative abilities.

This experience has shaped my interest in business by showing me the importance of strategic communication and branding in today’s digital world. It has also strengthened my problem-solving skills, as I often had to find creative solutions to present content in an engaging and professional way. I have learned how to adapt quickly, stay organized, and think critically about how to connect with an audience. As I continue my studies at UC Riverside, I am eager to expand my knowledge in areas such as marketing, finance, and business analytics. I am especially interested in exploring how data and technology can be used to inform business decisions and improve customer engagement. I look forward to gaining more experience through internships, student organizations, and collaborative projects. Overall, I am motivated to grow both academically and professionally, and I am excited to apply my skills and experiences in future opportunities within the business field.
